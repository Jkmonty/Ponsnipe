| `bow.glb` | Wooden Bow, 660 tris, no textures | Quaternius | CC0 1.0 (public domain) | https://poly.pizza/m/QnpqjLSKFU |

CC0 requires no attribution. This file exists so that the provenance of
anything shipped in the app is recorded, and so the next person adding a model
knows the bar: CC0 or CC-BY only, never non-commercial, since Ponsnipe charges
a trading fee and is therefore a commercial work.

Everything else in the range — trees, hedges, palisade, oaks, targets, the
castle — is generated in code in `src/app/arcade/world.ts`.
