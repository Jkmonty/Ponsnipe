Put the two brand images in this folder.

  mark.png       the glass P with the crosshair
  wordmark.png   "Ponsnipe" plus the tagline

PNG with a transparent background is best — the site is near-black, and a
white background baked into the file shows as a rectangle around the logo.
If you only have them on white, drop them in anyway and the background can
be keyed out.

Anything in public/ is served from the site root, so mark.png here becomes
/brand/mark.png in the page.
